Who this is for

Built for the moments that matter most in TA

Whether you're standing up a new ATS, documenting your process for the first time, or creating a deliverable for a client: this tool guides you through it.

🏗️

New ATS implementation

Your implementation consultant needs to understand your process before they can configure the system. This tool captures it in the format they need: before your kickoff call.

📄

First-time documentation

Many TA teams have never formally mapped their process: it lives in people's heads. This tool walks you through it step by step. No process design experience required.

🔄

Process redesign

Map the as-is state before designing the to-be. Identifying what you currently do is the necessary first step to improving it: use the output to facilitate a structured redesign.

🤝

Stakeholder alignment

Get recruiters, hiring managers, HR, and leadership on the same page about who does what and when. The swimlane map makes ownership visible in a way a conversation never can.

⚖️

Compliance & audit prep

Documenting your process for OFCCP, internal audit, or SOX requires a clear record of decision points, role assignments, and disposition procedures. The SOP output is built for this.

📊

Consultant deliverables

RPO, HR consulting, or implementation firms: run this on behalf of a client and deliver a branded, print-ready map and SOP as part of your engagement. White-labeling is built in.

⚠️
